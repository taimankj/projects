Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread (white, wheat, sourdough, or rye)
  • 3–4 slices deli ham (about 3 oz / 85 g)
  • 2 slices cheese (cheddar, Swiss, provolone, or American)
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard (optional)
  • 1 tbsp butter (if toasting)
  • 2–3 slices tomato (optional)
  • Lettuce leaves (optional)
  • Thinly sliced red onion (optional)
  • Black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Spread mayonnaise on one slice of bread.
  2. Spread Dijon mustard on the other slice (if using).
  3. Layer:
    • Ham
    • Cheese
    • Tomato
    • Lettuce
  4. Onion
  5. Sprinkle with black pepper.
  6. Top with the second slice of bread.
  7. Slice in half and serve.

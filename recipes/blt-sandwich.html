Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 4 slices bacon, cooked
  • 2–3 lettuce leaves
  • 2 slices tomato
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Toast the bread if desired.
  2. Spread mayonnaise on both slices.
  3. Layer the lettuce, tomato, and bacon.
  4. Season the tomato with salt and pepper.
  5. Top with the second slice, slice, and serve.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread or ciabatta
  • 4 oz cooked chicken breast, sliced
  • 2 tbsp basil pesto
  • 2 slices mozzarella or provolone
  • Tomato slices (optional)
  • Baby spinach (optional)

Steps

  1. Spread pesto on both slices of bread.
  2. Layer the chicken, cheese, tomato, and spinach.
  3. Toast or grill until golden and the cheese is melted, if desired.
  4. Slice and serve.
